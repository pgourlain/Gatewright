# Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review and merge changes, cut releases and enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). They are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
- **Contributors** are anyone who submits issues, pull requests, documentation or reviews.

## Decision making

- Day-to-day changes are decided by pull request review: one maintainer approval is required.
- Architectural decisions are recorded as ADRs in [docs/adr](docs/adr). A change that contradicts an accepted ADR requires a new ADR
  that supersedes it, and approval from the lead maintainer.
- Disagreements are resolved by discussion; if consensus is not reached, the lead maintainer decides.

## Adding and removing maintainers

- A contributor with a sustained record of quality contributions can be proposed by any maintainer; the lead maintainer decides after discussion.
- A maintainer can step down at any time. Maintainers inactive for 12 months may be moved to emeritus status.
